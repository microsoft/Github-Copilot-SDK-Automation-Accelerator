FRANKENSTEIN: CHAPTER-BY-CHAPTER OVERVIEW
==========================================

## FRAME NARRATIVE: WALTON'S LETTERS
**Letters 1-4**: Captain Walton's Arctic expedition; he rescues dying Victor and records his story

## VICTOR'S STORY: PART I - ORIGINS & CREATION (Chapters 1-5)
**Chapter 1**: Victor's wealthy Geneva family; adoption of Elizabeth Lavenza
**Chapter 2**: Childhood with Elizabeth and friend Henry Clerval; early interest in natural philosophy  
**Chapter 3**: Leaves for University of Ingolstadt; studies chemistry and anatomy
**Chapter 4**: Becomes obsessed with creating life; works secretly for two years
**Chapter 5**: **THE CREATION** - Brings creature to life; horrified, flees; falls ill

## PART II - CONSEQUENCES & FIRST MURDERS (Chapters 6-10)
**Chapter 6**: Recovers with Clerval's help; receives family news
**Chapter 7**: Brother William murdered; servant Justine accused
**Chapter 8**: Justine executed despite Victor's secret knowledge of creature's guilt
**Chapter 9**: Victor wracked with guilt; retreats to Alps
**Chapter 10**: Encounters creature on glacier; creature demands to tell his story

## PART III - THE CREATURE'S NARRATIVE (Chapters 11-16)
**Chapter 11**: Creature's first experiences - learning senses, discovering fire, food
**Chapter 12**: Finds hovel next to De Lacey cottage; begins observing family
**Chapter 13**: Continues watching De Laceys; learns language and human behavior  
**Chapter 14**: Learns their history through Safie's lessons
**Chapter 15**: Finds books (*Paradise Lost*, *Plutarch*, *Werther*); discovers Victor's journal
**Chapter 16**: Approaches De Laceys but violently rejected; burns cottage; kills William

## PART IV - DEMANDS & FINAL DESTRUCTION (Chapters 17-24)
**Chapter 17**: Creature demands Victor create female companion
**Chapter 18**: Victor travels with Clerval to England
**Chapter 19**: Works on female creature in remote Scottish lab
**Chapter 20**: Destroys unfinished female; creature vows revenge
**Chapter 21**: Clerval murdered; Victor accused but cleared
**Chapter 22**: Victor returns home; marries Elizabeth
**Chapter 23**: Creature murders Elizabeth on wedding night
**Chapter 24**: Victor pursues creature into Arctic; his story ends

## RETURN TO FRAME
**Walton's conclusion**: Victor dies; creature appears to mourn; vanishes into Arctic

## KEY TURNING POINTS
- **Chapter 5**: The creation - everything changes
- **Chapter 10**: Victor meets creature - central confrontation begins  
- **Chapter 16**: Creature's rejection by De Laceys - turns permanently evil
- **Chapter 20**: Victor destroys female - seals everyone's doom
- **Chapter 23**: Elizabeth's death - Victor's final loss
